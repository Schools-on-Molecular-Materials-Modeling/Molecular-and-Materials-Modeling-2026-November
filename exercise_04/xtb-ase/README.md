### Physical Breakdown of The GFN2-xTB Diels–Alder Results

* **Double-bond π → σ over-exothermicity:** Because the Diels–Alder reaction converts two C=C π-bonds into two new C–C σ-bonds (double the bond conversion of ethene hydrogenation, where GFN2-xTB had a -45 kJ/mol error), the minimal-basis σ-vs-π overbinding doubles to ~ -93 kJ/mol (-259.99 vs. -166.70 kJ/mol).
* **Accurate Zero-Point & Structural Trends:** The zero-point penalty (+19.60 kJ/mol) matches MOPAC PM7 (+20.87 kJ/mol), and the relaxed C=C double bonds reproduce the experimental ordering ($1.3285\text{ \AA}$ conjugated diene $> 1.3223\text{ \AA}$ cyclohexene $> 1.3164\text{ \AA}$ ethene).
