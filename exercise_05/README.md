# The Coronene Dimer

Coronene Dimer (C48H24, 72 Atoms) s an ideal benchmark system for testing non-covalent interactions.

• Pure dispersion regime: Two parallel, planar polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) have zero net dipole moment. Without an accurate treatment of dispersion physics, the predicted interaction energy is purely repulsive.

## Challenge

Repeat the potential energy surface calculations on the coronene dimer while adding finer sampling along the intermolecular distance (\(z\)-points). With the help of AI agents, evaluate and explain how accurately dispersion interactions are treated across xTB, MOPAC, and MACE methods.
